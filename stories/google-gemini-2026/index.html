Google's Gemini: from Gemini 3 to the Gemini 4 family

Google went from Gemini 3 to three Gemini 4 models in under a year, while pushing AI into Search, Android and free video tools.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Google launched Gemini 3 in November 2025, then a fast series of cheaper 'Flash' models, and in late 2026 the Gemini 4 family: Argon (30 September), Carbon (28 October) and the faster, cheaper Hydrogen (12 November).

Why it matters

Gemini sits inside Search, Android, Gmail and YouTube, so Google's models reach more people by default than any rival's.

What’s next

Wider Gemini 4 Carbon availability, and Hydrogen rolling out globally in Search and the Gemini app.

  10. 12 Nov2026
    12 Nov 2026 AI New since your last visit Confirmed · Google

    Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the new everyday model

    Google releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast, cheaper model built for AI agents that replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash as its everyday model. It is rolling out globally in Google Search for all users, in the Gemini app (free users get it through automatic model selection) and to developers at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than 3.8 Flash; these are Google's own figures.
