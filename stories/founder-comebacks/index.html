    Founders who left and came back: from Steve Jobs to Sam Altman

    Jobs, Schultz, Dell, Page, Schwab, Dorsey and Altman all left the top job and later returned. How each one left, how each came back, and Nvidia's open case.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    Some founders leave the top job and later return. Steve Jobs was pushed out of Apple in 1985 and came back in 1997. Howard Schultz returned to Starbucks twice. Michael Dell, Larry Page, Charles Schwab and Jack Dorsey also came back. Sam Altman was removed by OpenAI's board, and four days later it agreed to bring him back.

    Why it matters

    The way a founder leaves matters. Some stepped down by choice; others were removed by their boards, and accounts sometimes differ on which it was. Nvidia's Jensen Huang resigned on 12 November 2026 and has said he would return if asked; Nvidia's board chair says the board does not intend to reinstate him.

    What’s next

    Whether Nvidia's board changes its position on Huang, and who it picks as permanent CEO.
