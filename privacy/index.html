Privacy

Last updated 12 Nov 2026.

No accounts, no tracking cookies

You can read Newspin without signing up. Newspin does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

What your browser stores

When you follow a story, switch the detail level or open a timeline, Newspin saves that choice and the date in your browser’s local storage, so it can show you what changed since your last visit. This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers to fetch them.

Email alerts

If email alerts are offered and you sign up, we use your email address only to send updates on the stories you chose, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
