    US markets: the daily wrap

    How the S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq opened and closed each trading day, the gap at the open, and the reasons reporters gave. Dates are US dates; the close comes at 2:30 am IST.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    The S&P 500 fell 0.50% to 7,201.09 on Tuesday 24 November and the Nasdaq fell 1.48%, as chip and AI shares gave back part of Monday's rally. The Dow rose 0.85% to 50,018.70, helped by retailers and stronger consumer confidence. On Monday the S&P 500 had jumped 4.73% after Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO.

    Why it matters

    Doubts about AI spending, reports of new US tariffs and expectations of a Fed rate rise in December are weighing on tech shares. Big US moves often set the tone for Indian markets the next trading day.

    What’s next

    The PCE inflation report is due on 25 November (US time), the last major data release before the Fed's December meeting, per Reuters.

    11. 23 Nov2026
      23 Nov 2026 Markets New since your last visit Market data · Investopedia/Yahoo Finance, FinanceFeeds

      S&P 500 jumps 4.7% after Nvidia brings back Jensen Huang, ending a seven-day losing run

      The S&P 500 rose 4.73% to 7,237.17, its best day since April 2025, per Investopedia (via Yahoo Finance). Its 327-point rise was its second-biggest ever in points. The Dow rose 3.01% to 49,595.31 and the Nasdaq 6.51% to 24,089.56. Before the open, Nvidia said its board had reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO. Nvidia shares rose about 31%, their biggest one-day gain since 2003, and added about $950 billion in market value. Reporters also pointed to forced buying by traders who had bet on further falls, lower oil prices, and Oracle saying it has the funding it needs for its OpenAI computing deal. A Nomura strategist called it a "mechanical" bounce that does not settle worries about inflation, borrowing costs and AI spending. Anthropic shares fell on the day, by 1.43% per CNBC. Exact opening levels were not given, so this day has no gap figure; the SPY fund that tracks the S&P 500 opened about 5% higher, per Yahoo Finance.

    12. 24 Nov2026
      24 Nov 2026 Markets New since your last visit Market data · AP, BigGo Finance, Reuters

      Nasdaq falls 1.5% as the Nvidia rally fades; Dow rises 0.9% and closes above 50,000

      The S&P 500 fell 0.50% to 7,201.09 and the Nasdaq 1.48% to 23,733.54, per AP (via La Nación) and BigGo Finance. The Dow rose 0.85% to 50,018.70. Chip and AI shares gave back part of Monday's rally. Nvidia fell 5.86% to $155.38, per Reuters data via MarketScreener, and the Philadelphia chip index fell 7.88%, per BigGo Finance. One analyst told Reuters that "one headline doesn't really resolve investor questions about the AI trade." Reports that the US may soon raise tariffs on China and other trading partners also weighed on shares, per TipRanks and Reuters. Retailers rose after strong results: Dollar Tree gained about 11%. A Conference Board survey showed consumer confidence rose to 103.3 in November, above the 92.5 economists expected, which helped the Dow. Anthropic shares fell below their $83 IPO price; TipRanks puts the day's fall at 8%. Traders expect the Fed to raise rates by 0.25 percentage points in December, per the CME FedWatch tool. Exact opening levels were not given, so this day has no gap figure; at 10:05 am the S&P 500 was down 0.5% after falling as much as 1.4%, per AP.
