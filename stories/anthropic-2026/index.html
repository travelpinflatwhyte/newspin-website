Anthropic: every Claude model and the road to an IPO

From Claude 4 to the Mythos-class models, a pause under US export controls, and a Nasdaq listing set for next week.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Anthropic released Claude 4 in May 2025, then a new top model every few months, adding a 'Mythos' tier above Opus in 2026. It filed publicly for an IPO in October and began marketing its shares on 10 November.

Why it matters

Claude is one of the leading AI assistants, especially for coding and business use, and Anthropic's listing is a test of how investors value AI labs.

What’s next

Pricing is expected on 18 November and trading on 19 November, subject to regulatory clearance.
