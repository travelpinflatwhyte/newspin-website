What Newspin is

Newspin organises reporting on major ongoing news stories into timelines. Each event summarises, in our own words, what published sources have reported, and links to those sources so you can read them yourself.

Not advice

Everything on Newspin is general information. It is not financial, investment, legal, tax or medical advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. Make important decisions using the original sources and, where appropriate, a qualified professional.

Accuracy

We work to keep timelines accurate and current: every event is sourced, reports are labelled by how firm they are (confirmed, reported, allegation, forecast), and disagreements between outlets are flagged. News changes quickly, though, and sources can be wrong or updated after we read them. Newspin is provided as it is, without guarantees of completeness or accuracy. Allegations described on Newspin are claims made by the people or outlets named, not findings of fact.

How Newspin is made

Newspin uses AI tools to help find news, draft summaries and update timelines, working to written editorial rules. Updates may be published automatically. Every event links to its sources so you can check it.

Corrections and complaints

If you spot an error, or believe something on Newspin is unfair or unlawful, email hello@newspinapp.com with the page link and what is wrong. We review every report and correct mistakes openly on the timeline.

Using Newspin

You are welcome to read, link to and share Newspin pages. Please do not copy timelines wholesale, republish them or scrape the site for commercial use without permission.

Other people’s content and names

Linked articles and documents belong to their publishers. Company and product names belong to their owners and are used only to identify them; Newspin is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Changes

We may update these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use Newspin means you accept the current terms.
