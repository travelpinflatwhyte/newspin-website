    Apple: from a garage to $5 trillion

    Fifty years of Apple: the founding, the boardroom fight that pushed Steve Jobs out, his return, the iPhone, the Tim Cook years and the new CEO.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    Apple was founded in a California garage in 1976. Steve Jobs was pushed out in 1985, came back in 1997 when the company was struggling, and led it through the iPod, iPhone and iPad. Tim Cook ran it from 2011 to 2026, and John Ternus took over on 1 September 2026.

    Why it matters

    Apple's products changed how people use computers, music and phones, and it is now the world's most valuable company. Its history also includes a founder who was pushed out and later came back to lead a recovery.

    What’s next

    Ternus faces questions about Apple's place in AI and how well the foldable iPhone Duo sells. Cook stays on as executive chairman.
