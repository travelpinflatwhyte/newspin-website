West Asia war, the Strait of Hormuz and India’s oil bill

How the US–Iran conflict disrupted the world’s most important oil route, and why it matters so much to India.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Since the war began, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz has been disrupted. Brent peaked near $118 in late October, then fell sharply in November as Qatar mediated talks between Iran and the US, before rebounding on fresh attacks.

Why it matters

India imports most of its crude oil, and a large share of its oil, LNG and almost all its LPG has passed through Hormuz. The government says it absorbed ₹43 of a needed ₹50-a-litre diesel increase.

What’s next

Oil posted a second weekly fall, and the IEA expects a supply surplus this quarter. But tanker and Houthi attacks keep prices jumpy, and on 12 November President Trump said the US did not need a deal with Iran.
