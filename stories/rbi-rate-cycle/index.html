RBI’s interest-rate cycle: from five cuts to its first hike in over three years

How the Reserve Bank went from cutting rates to raising them in 20 months, and what it means for your loans and the economy.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

On 7 Oct 2026 the RBI raised its key rate to 5.50%, its first increase since February 2023, after cutting it five times in 2025.

Why it matters

Inflation picked up sharply through mid-2026. Borrowers with repo-linked floating loans may see higher EMIs at their next reset.

What’s next

The next policy meeting is 2–4 Dec 2026. The new “calibrated tightening” stance leaves room for another hike.

What this means for you

Home and car loans linked to the repo rate may cost more at their next reset, depending on your loan terms. Fixed-rate loans you already have are not affected.
