xAI and Grok: models, the SpaceX merger and the missing Grok 5

Elon Musk's AI company shipped Grok 3 to Grok 4.7, merged into SpaceX and bought Cursor. Grok 5 has slipped past every promised date.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

xAI released Grok 3 in February 2025 and Grok 4 in July 2025, then a run of Grok 4.x models through 2026. SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026, and the company now uses SpaceX branding.

Why it matters

Grok is built into X and Tesla products, and xAI's huge Colossus computers make it one of the few labs able to train the largest models.

What’s next

Grok 5: xAI said in January it was in training. Targets of late 2025, early 2026 and mid-2026 all passed; SpaceX has since pointed to before the end of 2026.
