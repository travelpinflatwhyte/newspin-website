      Two tankers hit leaving Hormuz; Iran fires missiles and drones at US bases in Bahrain

      Early on Saturday two tankers leaving the Gulf were hit by unknown projectiles, one about 20 km north of Khasab in Oman and one about 31 km east of it, said UKMTO, the British agency that tracks shipping. One was hit in the stern and one in the engine room; no one was reported hurt. Iran's navy said a tanker caught fire after hitting a mine in a restricted zone east of the strait; it is not clear if this was one of the two. On Saturday night Iran launched drones and ballistic missiles at US bases in Bahrain, per Iranian media cited by Euronews. Bahrain said its air defences intercepted several attacks and a fire broke out at a civilian industrial site, with no injuries. The Revolutionary Guards (IRGC) said they hit two Patriot missile batteries and a radar; Bahrain and the US have not confirmed this. The US military denied a separate IRGC claim that Iran damaged a US F-16 jet on Friday. Iran's deputy foreign minister said more than 14 Iranian tankers are ready to sail through the US blockade to China, and warned of a 'fierce military response' if they are stopped.
