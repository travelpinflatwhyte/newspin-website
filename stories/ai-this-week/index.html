AI this week: the developments that matter

New models, money, lawsuits and leadership changes at OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia and others. The AI news worth knowing, updated with each refresh.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

In the past ten days Google, Meta and Anthropic released new models, Anthropic's share sale drew strong reported demand ahead of a Nasdaq listing, OpenAI let ChatGPT agents make purchases, Nvidia's founder was forced out, and nonprofits asked a court to freeze frontier AI.

Why it matters

AI is now in phones, offices and government services. Who builds it, who pays for it and what rules apply decide what you can use, what it costs and how safe it is.

Nvidia reports earnings on 17 Nov. Anthropic expects to price its IPO on 18 Nov and list on 19 Nov.

    Nonprofits sue to freeze frontier AI

    Four nonprofits, including Public Citizen, sue Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI in San Francisco, asking a judge to stop them building or releasing more capable AI. They argue the risks break California's unfair competition law. The claims have not been tested in court, no freeze has been ordered, and the companies had not commented.
