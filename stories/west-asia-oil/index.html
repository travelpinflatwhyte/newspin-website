West Asia war, the Strait of Hormuz and India’s oil bill

How the US–Iran conflict disrupted the world’s most important oil route, and why it matters so much to India.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Since strikes on Iran in March 2026, shipping through the Strait of Hormuz has been disrupted and oil prices have swung between about $80 and over $100.

Why it matters

India imports most of its crude oil, and a large share of its oil, LNG and almost all its LPG has passed through Hormuz.

What’s next

Any reopening of Hormuz shipping lanes, or a lasting ceasefire, would ease prices quickly.
