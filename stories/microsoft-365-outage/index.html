    Outlook and Teams down: the Microsoft 365 outage, hour by hour

    What broke, when (in India time), what Microsoft has said, and what still works.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    From late on 12 November (India time), Microsoft's Exchange Online servers failed, taking down Outlook and parts of Teams, SharePoint and Copilot in 74 countries and territories for about a day. Microsoft's preliminary report blames a hidden bug in a months-old fix to its gateway servers, which surfaced after Teams traffic was moved onto them while some capacity was already offline. It says it was not a cyber attack.

    Why it matters

    Microsoft 365 runs email and meetings for millions of companies, schools and government offices, including in India. Courts lost live feeds and Australian agencies were hit.

    What’s next

    Microsoft's final report, published on 20 November, says a months-old gateway bug, capacity offline for maintenance, Teams traffic moved onto the email gateway and peak demand combined. It plans separate gateway capacity for different services by early 2027.

      What caused it: early explanations

      Microsoft has traced the trouble to an overloaded process on affected servers that sets up secure connections, which caused them to fail health checks. The Verge's Tom Warren reports it is linked to an Azure network (VNET) configuration change, and Australia's Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke says Microsoft told him it was related to a software update. Microsoft has not confirmed a root cause and has promised a post-incident report.

    10. 15 Nov2026
      15 Nov 2026 Tech New since your last visit Stated · Microsoft (preliminary report)

      Microsoft's preliminary report: a months-old hidden bug

      In a preliminary incident report for customers, Microsoft says three things combined. A "latent defect" in a fix rolled out from July to the servers that route traffic into Microsoft 365 made them freeze under certain loads. About 15 hours earlier, Teams traffic had been moved onto the same gateway that serves Exchange Online email. And nearly 14% of the gateway's servers were already out of service for an unrelated reason. Within two hours the fault spread across the fleet, and at worst more than 80% of servers stopped responding. Microsoft puts the outage at 4:20 pm UTC on 12 Nov (9:50 pm IST) to 4:10 pm UTC on 13 Nov (9:40 pm IST). It says the defect does not affect ordinary Windows computers, that it has added capacity and guardrails, and that a final report will follow.

    11. 20 Nov2026
      20 Nov 2026 Tech New since your last visit Confirmed · Microsoft (via third-party summaries of its report)

      Microsoft's final report: four problems at once caused the outage

      Microsoft published its final post-incident report to business customers on 20 November, replacing the preliminary one, per summaries by 365fanatic and AppleScoop. It says four things combined: a defect in a gateway update that had been rolling out since July, about 14% of gateway capacity being offline for routine maintenance, Teams traffic moving onto the gateway shared with Exchange Online email, and peak North American demand. At the worst point more than 80% of servers stopped responding, and the failure reached every region within about two hours. Load tests missed the bug because they ran on older machines with fewer processor cores. Microsoft found no malicious activity, said AI tools did not make the faulty change, and plans to give different services separate gateway capacity by early 2027.

    What this means for you

    Outlook and Teams should be working again. Emails sent during the outage may arrive late as queues clear. If something still fails, restart the app or sign out and back in.
