India’s online gaming crackdown and its new regulator

Real-money games are banned. A new authority now decides what counts as esports or a social game.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Since 1 May 2026, online games with money stakes are banned, and the Online Gaming Authority of India classifies and registers games.

Why it matters

It reshaped one of India’s fastest-growing digital industries, from fantasy sports to esports and game studios.

What this means for you

Games with no money at stake can keep running without mandatory registration unless the authority asks. Platforms must store Indian users’ data in India and offer age checks and parental controls.
