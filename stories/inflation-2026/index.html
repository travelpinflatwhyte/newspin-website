Inflation is back: India’s climb from record lows

Retail inflation fell to its lowest ever in late 2025. Within a year it was above 5% again. Here is how it happened.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Retail inflation rose to 5.54% in September 2026, with food inflation at 7.74%. A year earlier it was close to zero.

Why it matters

Higher prices for food and fuel squeeze household budgets, and rising inflation pushed the RBI into its first rate hike since 2023.

What’s next

October’s figure is released on 12 November 2026. ICRA expects it to cross 6% (a forecast).
