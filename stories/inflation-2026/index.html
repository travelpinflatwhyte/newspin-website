Inflation is back: India’s climb from record lows

Retail inflation fell to its lowest ever in late 2025. By October 2026 it had crossed the RBI's 6% upper limit. Here is how it happened.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Retail inflation rose to 6.09% in October 2026, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit for the first time in two years. Food inflation reached 8.94%.

Why it matters

Higher prices for food and fuel squeeze household budgets. Inflation above 6% puts pressure on the RBI to raise rates again.

What’s next

The RBI's next policy decision comes after its 2–4 Dec meeting. November inflation is due on 12 December.
