  1. Lift-off. Starship launches from Starbase in Texas on the evening of 13 November.
  2. Hot staging. A few minutes in, the ship lights its own engines while still attached, then separates from the booster.
  3. The booster's plan. Turn around with an engine burn, fly back and be caught by the tower's arms within minutes of launch.
  4. What happened. A low-fuel alarm called off the catch and the booster was sent to the Gulf of Mexico instead.
  5. Into orbit. The ship reaches orbit and releases 26 Starlink V3 internet satellites.
  6. Around Earth. It keeps circling the planet. On the drawing, its path leaves on the right and comes back in on the left.
  7. Deorbit burn. A single engine fires to slow the ship down so it starts falling back.
  8. Re-entry. It drops through the atmosphere belly first, heat shield facing down, steering with its four flaps.
  9. Flip and catch. Near the tower it flips upright, fires its engines to slow down, and the tower's arms ("chopsticks") catch it at 3:20 pm Texas time on 14 November (2:50 am IST, 15 November), about 22.5 hours after launch. SpaceX says it is the first time it has caught an upper stage.
