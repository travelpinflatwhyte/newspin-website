Outlook and Teams down: the Microsoft 365 outage, hour by hour

What broke, when (in India time), what Microsoft has said, and what still works.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

From late on 12 November (India time), Microsoft's Exchange Online servers failed, taking down Outlook and parts of Teams, SharePoint and Copilot in 74 countries and territories for about a day. Microsoft says a problem introduced during an earlier network capacity upgrade surfaced after a recent network change, and that it was not a cyber attack.

Why it matters

Microsoft 365 runs email and meetings for millions of companies, schools and government offices, including in India. Courts lost live feeds and Australian agencies were hit.

What’s next

Microsoft reports broad recovery and has apologised. Its full incident report should give more detail on what went wrong.

    What caused it: early explanations

    Microsoft has traced the trouble to an overloaded process on affected servers that sets up secure connections, which caused them to fail health checks. The Verge's Tom Warren reports it is linked to an Azure network (VNET) configuration change, and Australia's Home Affairs Minister Tony Burke says Microsoft told him it was related to a software update. Microsoft has not confirmed a root cause and has promised a post-incident report.
