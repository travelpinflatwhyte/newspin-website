The rupee in 2026: a record slide, then a rebound

From near 89 to the dollar in January to a record low near 96.90, then a rebound in November. What moved the rupee this year.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

The rupee hit a string of record lows in 2026, bottoming near 96.90 per dollar. Since October it has recovered more than 3%, reaching a six-month high of 93.70 on 9 November.

Why it matters

A weaker rupee makes imports like oil, electronics and foreign travel or education more expensive, and adds to inflation.

What’s next

Falling oil prices, foreign inflows and RBI dollar sales are helping. A renewed oil spike would reverse that.

What this means for you

Anything priced in dollars costs more: foreign trips, overseas tuition, imported gadgets. Families receiving money from abroad get more rupees per dollar or dirham.
