    Russia–Ukraine peace talks: from Abu Dhabi to the Crimean bridge strikes

    US-brokered talks restarted in November after nine months and agreed a big prisoner swap. Then Ukraine hit the Crimean bridge, Putin hardened his line and Russia struck Kyiv's bridges.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    The US brokered three-way talks with Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi in January and February 2026, then they stalled for nine months. They resumed on 10–11 November and agreed a large prisoner exchange, but not the main dispute: Russia wants all of Ukraine's Donbas region. In the week after, Ukraine struck the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea three times. On 20 November Russia hit three bridges in Kyiv; Ukraine said eight people were killed.

    Why it matters

    It is Europe's largest war since 1945. Its course affects energy and food prices, sanctions on Russia (including on the oil India buys) and European security.

    What’s next

    Putin said on 19 November there would be no talks on energy or Black Sea ceasefires after the bridge strikes, and Russia hit Kyiv's bridges a day later. US envoy Kushner says Russia accepts four-way talks with Europe; Moscow has not confirmed this. No date is set. The agreed prisoner swap has not yet been reported as carried out.

    1. 23–24 Jan2026
      23–24 Jan 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · US, Russian and Ukrainian delegations

      First US–Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi end without a deal

      The United Arab Emirates hosted the first three-way talks, brokered by the US. Russia's team was led by its military intelligence chief and Ukraine's by Rustem Umerov. There was no agreement: Russia demanded that Ukraine withdraw fully from the Donbas region in the east, and Kyiv refused. US officials said the talks 'surpassed expectations'. On the night they ended, Russia launched more than 370 drones and 21 missiles at Ukraine, per CNN.

    2. 4–5 Feb2026
      4–5 Feb 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Witkoff, Russian Defence Ministry

      Second round: no ceasefire, but 314 prisoners of war swapped

      A second round in Abu Dhabi produced no ceasefire; control of the Donetsk region remained the main dispute. US envoy Steve Witkoff said the sides agreed to exchange 314 prisoners of war, and Russia's RIA news agency reported 157 returned by each side. It was the first swap since October 2025. Talks then stalled and lapsed after the US and Israel launched a war on Iran, Al Jazeera and Reuters report.

    5. 10–11 Nov2026
      10–11 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Russian and Ukrainian delegations

      First three-way talks in nine months: a big prisoner swap agreed, no breakthrough

      Kushner and Witkoff mediated, with Vladimir Medinsky leading Russia's team and Umerov Ukraine's. Umerov said the main result was agreement on a 'large-scale prisoner exchange'; Medinsky called it a 'fundamental agreement'. A Russian general quoted by the TASS news agency said it would involve more than 1,000 people on each side. Kushner said the gaps had 'narrowed', but the sides still disagreed on territory, including Russia's demand for all of Donbas.

      US envoys meet Putin in Moscow, then Zelenskyy

      Witkoff and Kushner met Putin in the Kremlin for about two hours; his aide Yuri Ushakov called the talks 'substantive, constructive, frank and useful', AP reports. Putin said European troops in Ukraine after a ceasefire would be 'absolutely unacceptable'. Russian state TV quoted Witkoff as saying 'the Europeans will join at the next meeting'. The envoys then met Zelenskyy in Dublin.

      16–17 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Ukrainian General Staff, Russian-installed Crimea authorities

      Ukrainian strike brings down part of the Crimean bridge road

      Ukraine hit the 19-km Kerch bridge, which links Russia to occupied Crimea, with drones and missiles. Zelenskyy called it an 'unprecedented operation in response to Russian attacks on our infrastructure'. Al Jazeera reports about 200 metres of roadway heavily damaged and partly underwater. Russia opened a terrorism case, and the Kremlin said the bridge would be restored. Russian strikes damaged two bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv the same day.

    10. 19 Nov2026
      19 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Stated · Putin

      Putin: 'After the strikes on the Crimean bridge, there will be no negotiations'

      At a press conference after APEC, Putin said Moscow would no longer discuss ceasefires covering energy sites and Black Sea shipping, and promised a response 'far more painful'. Zelenskyy said Russia rejects every proposal to de-escalate. The wider US-led talks have not been formally called off. In Berlin, European leaders and the US envoys agreed that national security advisers from Britain, France, Germany, Italy, Poland and the EU would represent Europe if the talks widen, The National reports; Kushner said 'President Trump wants to make a deal with Europe by his side.'

    11. 19–20 Nov2026
      19–20 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Stated · Kushner; Stated · Russian Foreign Ministry

      Kushner says Russia accepts Europe at the table; Moscow rejects any NATO-style role for Kyiv

      After the Berlin meeting of Ukraine's allies, US envoy Jared Kushner said: 'I think we've gotten the Russians comfortable with that concept where any security guarantee for Ukraine would be based on Article 5.' Article 5 is NATO's rule that an attack on one member is an attack on all. He also said Russia had agreed to talks that include Europe. Moscow has not confirmed this. Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Sergei Ryabkov said any 'quasi membership' of NATO for Kyiv was 'totally unacceptable', and that a visit by European representatives was 'under consideration'. Zelenskyy said the next meeting would be four-way: Ukraine, the US, Russia and Europe. No date has been set.

    12. 20 Nov2026
      20 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Kyiv city authorities, Ukrainian government, Russian Defence Ministry

      Russia strikes three Kyiv bridges after Putin promises a response

      Overnight Russian ballistic missiles damaged the Podilskyi, Darnytskyi and Southern bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said. Traffic was restricted and people crossed the river on foot. Prime Minister Koretskyi said eight people were killed, six of them in Kyiv, and 28 wounded. Russia's Defence Ministry said it targeted bridges used to move weapons, plus defence, energy and transport sites. Power was cut across Kyiv and in 10 regions after nuclear plants reduced output, Ukraine's energy ministry said. Putin had said in China: 'We will respond to these attacks.'

      Night of 20 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Kryvyi Rih city and regional officials (via AP)

      Russian strike on Kryvyi Rih, Zelenskyy's hometown, kills two

      A Russian strike hit the centre of Kryvyi Rih on Friday night. A 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman were killed, the head of the city administration said. The regional military administration said 60 people were wounded, including six children. City officials said about 25 apartment buildings, a hospital and schools were damaged. Asked about Trump calling Putin his "friend", Zelenskyy told CNN that for Ukraine Putin is "an enemy". There is still no date for a new round of talks.
