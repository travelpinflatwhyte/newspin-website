India–US trade deal: announced in February, still not signed

A tariff cut was announced with fanfare. Nine months later, courts, new duties and a sanctions law have kept it in limbo.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

The US and India announced a deal in February to cut US tariffs on Indian goods to 18% from 50%. As of 3 November there is still no signed agreement.

Why it matters

US tariffs decide how competitive Indian exports like textiles, gems, engineering goods and pharma are in India’s biggest export market.

What’s next

No public deadline. Some trade analysts say the US midterm results make an early interim deal more likely.
