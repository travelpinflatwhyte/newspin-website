The FCRA Amendment Bill: tighter rules for foreign-funded NGOs

A bill that would let the government take over assets of NGOs that lose their foreign-funding licence is now with a parliamentary committee.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

The Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026 is being examined by a 31-member joint parliamentary committee. It is not yet law.

Why it matters

It would affect thousands of NGOs, charities and religious bodies that receive foreign funding, including what happens to their assets if they lose registration.

What’s next

The committee’s report is due by the end of the first week of the 2026 Winter Session.

What this means for you

If you run or work with an FCRA-registered organisation, the current Act and the 2026 Rules still apply. The bill changes nothing until Parliament passes it.
