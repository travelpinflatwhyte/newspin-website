SpiceJet's troubles: cancellations, unpaid dues and a search for money

How one of India's oldest budget airlines came to cancel one in five flights, what it owes, and what its board will consider on 18 November.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

SpiceJet has been cancelling about one in five of its flights since August, according to official data. It is down to 12 aircraft, three of them reportedly grounded, and media reports say it is behind on payments to suppliers, aircraft lessors and staff. It has not published financial results since the December 2025 quarter.

Why it matters

Passengers face last-minute cancellations, long delays and slow refunds. The aviation minister said in September that the regulator was reviewing SpiceJet's operations and finances.

What’s next

SpiceJet's board meets on 18 November to consider raising fresh capital through shares, debt or a share sale to institutions.

What this means for you

Booked on SpiceJet? Check your flight status on the day and keep proof of any cancellation or long delay. If SpiceJet cancels your flight, ask for a refund or a seat on another flight; DGCA rules set deadlines for airlines to pay refunds.
