US midterms 2026: from election night to a new Congress

The results so far, the races still being counted, and what changes when the new Congress meets in January.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Americans voted on 3 November for all 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats. As of 13 November, AP's count shows Democrats with 255 House seats won and Republicans with 169, and 55 Senate seats for Democrats (including 2 who caucus with them) against 44 for Republicans.

Why it matters

Congress writes US spending and trade laws and confirms judges and officials, so the result shapes the last two years of President Trump's term, including tariff fights that matter to Indian exporters.

What’s next

Remaining races are still being counted. The new Congress is due to meet in early January 2027.

    Election day, record midterm turnout

    Voters choose all 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats, plus governors. More than 124 million ballots are cast, above the previous midterm record of nearly 119 million in 2018, according to the University of Florida Election Lab.

What this means for you

For India, the result matters most through trade: tariffs, the unsigned India–US deal and sanctions on Russian oil all pass through Washington. Commerce ministry sources say the result will not affect the interim trade deal.
