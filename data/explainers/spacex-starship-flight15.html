  • The booster's planned return
  1. Lift-off. Starship launches from Starbase in Texas on the evening of 13 November, aiming to bring both stages back to the tower.
  2. Hot staging. A few minutes in, the ship lights its own engines while still attached, then separates from the booster.
  3. Booster diverted. The booster was meant to fly back and be caught. A low-fuel alarm called that off and it was sent to the Gulf of Mexico.
  4. Ship in orbit. The ship reaches orbit, releases 26 Starlink V3 internet satellites and stays up for about 22.5 hours.
  5. Ship caught. At 3:20 pm Texas time on 14 November (2:50 am IST, 15 November) it flies back and the tower's arms, nicknamed "chopsticks", catch it. SpaceX says it is the first time it has caught an upper stage.
