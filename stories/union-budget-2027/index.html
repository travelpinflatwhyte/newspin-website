The road to Budget 2027: what’s happened since the last Budget

From the February 2026 Budget to a fuel-tax cut, a new Income Tax Act and a widening deficit: everything that shapes the Budget due on 1 February 2027.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

The last Budget, on 1 February 2026, aimed for a fiscal deficit of 4.3% of GDP and brought in a new Income Tax Act from April. Since then a ₹10-a-litre fuel tax cut and higher spending have pushed the deficit up faster than last year, and work on Budget 2027-28 began in August.

Why it matters

The Budget sets income tax, fuel duties, government spending and borrowing for the year. This one has to balance a bigger deficit, oil-price shocks and the coming 8th Pay Commission bill.

What’s next

Ministry-level budget meetings were scheduled to end in mid-November. Final spending figures are due by early January, and the Budget is likely to be presented on 1 February 2027.

What this means for you

Income tax rates were unchanged in the last Budget, and the new Income Tax Act has applied since April 2026. For Budget 2027, watch for any change to tax slabs, fuel duties, and money set aside for the 8th Pay Commission, which matters to about 1.1 crore central government employees and pensioners.
