OpenAI: every GPT model, the money and the IPO

From GPT-4.5 to GPT-6 in 19 months, plus a confidential IPO filing, billions in losses and a race with Anthropic.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

OpenAI moved from GPT-5 in August 2025 to the GPT-6 family in September 2026, releasing a new GPT-5.x version roughly every quarter in between. It filed confidentially for an IPO in June 2026, but Sam Altman later ruled out listing this year.

Why it matters

ChatGPT is the most widely used AI assistant, so OpenAI's choices on models, prices and safety set the pace for the whole industry.

What’s next

An IPO is now expected in 2027. Watch for GPT-6 updates and how OpenAI responds to Anthropic's listing next week.
