India's AI rules: from guidelines to a draft law

India chose light-touch AI guidelines in 2025. A year later the government is preparing draft rules, funding homegrown models and asking where Indians' data goes.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

India published voluntary AI governance guidelines in November 2025 and hosted a global AI summit in February 2026. In October, IT Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw promised a consultation paper on AI rules covering safety and deepfakes, and the government is backing 20 homegrown AI models.

Why it matters

The rules will decide how deepfakes are handled, what AI apps must tell you, how your data can be used to train AI, and how easily Indian startups can compete.

What’s next

The consultation paper was promised within a month of 8 October and is expected in November. Draft rules are open to public comment once published.
