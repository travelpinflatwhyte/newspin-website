The rupee’s record slide in 2026

From near 89 to the dollar in January to above 96 by May. Why the rupee became Asia’s worst-performing currency this year.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

The rupee has lost close to 8% against the dollar in 2026, setting record lows again and again.

Why it matters

A weaker rupee makes imports like oil, electronics and foreign travel or education more expensive, and adds to inflation.

What’s next

Watch oil prices and foreign investor flows. The RBI has been selling dollars to slow the fall rather than defend a level.

What this means for you

Anything priced in dollars costs more: foreign trips, overseas tuition, imported gadgets. Families receiving money from abroad get more rupees per dollar or dirham.
