India’s Russian oil dilemma

India promised Washington it would stop buying Russian oil. Then war closed the Gulf’s main shipping route.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

India agreed to halt Russian oil purchases as part of the February US trade deal, but the Hormuz crisis pushed refiners back toward Russian crude.

Why it matters

Cheap Russian oil cushions India’s import bill; losing it, or being punished for it, would raise costs and strain relations with the US.

What’s next

Whether the US applies the new sanctions tariffs to India.
