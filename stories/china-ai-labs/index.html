China's AI labs: DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi and the open-model race

Cheap, open models from DeepSeek, Alibaba, Moonshot and Zhipu closed much of the gap with US labs, and sparked accusations of copying.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

DeepSeek's R1 shook the industry in January 2025. In 2026 Chinese labs released a wave of large open-weight models, including DeepSeek V4, Moonshot's Kimi K3, Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max and Zhipu's GLM-5.2, often far cheaper than US rivals.

Why it matters

Open, cheap models let anyone build AI products without paying US companies, and they sit at the centre of the US–China technology contest.

What’s next

Alibaba says Qwen 4 is coming 'very soon', and DeepSeek has confirmed a V4.1 Pro, without dates.

What this means for you

Chinese models are free to download and run, and some apps you use may be built on them. Their chatbots follow Chinese content rules, which shape answers on some political topics.
