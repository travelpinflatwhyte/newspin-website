    India markets: the daily wrap

    How the Nifty 50 and Sensex opened and closed each trading day, the overnight gap, and the reasons reporters gave.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    The Nifty 50 closed at 25,387.40 on 17 November after a flat open, its second gain in a row. Over six sessions since the Diwali Muhurat trading close on 8 November, it rose from 24,986.40.

    Why it matters

    Reporters credit heavy foreign investor buying and falling oil prices for the run, while October retail inflation of 6.09% caused the one big down day, on 13 November.

    What’s next

    Markets are shut on 24 November for Guru Nanak Jayanti. The RBI's next policy meeting runs 2 to 4 December.
