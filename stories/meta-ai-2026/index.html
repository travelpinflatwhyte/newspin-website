Meta's AI reset: from Llama 4 to Muse

After Llama 4 disappointed, Meta spent billions on a new superintelligence lab, dropped the Llama name and shipped the Muse models.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Llama 4 launched in April 2025 amid a benchmark controversy. Meta then invested about $14 bn in Scale AI, set up Meta Superintelligence Labs under Alexandr Wang, and from April 2026 released the closed Muse Spark, open Muse Glimmer and, in October, Muse Wonder, which Mark Zuckerberg called Meta's first frontier model.

Why it matters

Meta AI is built into WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, used by billions, and Meta's shift away from fully open models changes what developers can freely use.

What’s next

How Muse Wonder performs, and whether Meta keeps releasing open-weight models.
