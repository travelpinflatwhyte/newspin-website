Outlook and Teams down: the Microsoft 365 outage, hour by hour

What broke, when (in India time), what Microsoft has said, and what still works.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

From late on 12 November (India time), Microsoft's Exchange Online servers began failing, taking down Outlook email and parts of Teams, SharePoint and Copilot worldwide. Servers that came back kept failing again under traffic.

Why it matters

Microsoft 365 runs email and meetings for millions of companies, schools and government offices, including in India. Courts lost live feeds and Australian agencies were hit.

What’s next

As of 10 am IST on 13 November, Microsoft said it had not seen a sustained recovery. It has not given a time for a full fix or a final cause.

What this means for you

If you use Outlook for work or college, email may be delayed rather than lost, Microsoft says. Teams chat and meetings mostly work; calendar and scheduling may fail. Microsoft posts official updates on its @MSFT365Status account on X.
