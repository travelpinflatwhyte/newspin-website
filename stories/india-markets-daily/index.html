    India markets: the daily wrap

    How the Nifty 50 and Sensex opened and closed each trading day, the overnight gap, and the reasons reporters gave.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    The Nifty 50 closed at 25,800.60 on 20 November, up 1.12%, after opening about 146 points lower. HDFC Sky calls it a nine-month high. Over nine sessions since the Diwali Muhurat trading close on 8 November, it is up from 24,986.40.

    Why it matters

    The day started lower after a sell-off in AI-linked technology shares in the US and Asia. Reporters credit the recovery to foreign investor buying, bank and IT stocks, lower oil prices and a stronger rupee. Nifty Bank closed at a record.

    What’s next

    Markets are shut on 24 November for Guru Nanak Jayanti. The RBI's next policy meeting runs 2 to 4 December.

      Banks pull Nifty back from a weak open to close above 25,800

      ▼ Gap down −146.30Closed higher
      Previous close25,514.30
      Open25,368.00 −146.30
      Close25,800.60 +286.30 (+1.12%)

      The Nifty opened about 146 points lower at 25,368, according to Upstox and Liquide, after a sell-off in AI-linked technology shares overnight in the US and Asia. It then recovered to close up 286.30 points (1.12%) at 25,800.60, according to Business Today, Kotak Neo, HDFC Sky and Liquide. The Sensex rose 897.96 points (1.08%) to 83,919.71. HDFC Sky calls it a nine-month high. Reporters credit buying by foreign investors, gains in bank and IT stocks, lower oil prices and a stronger rupee. HDFC Sky says global funds are moving money out of AI-linked stocks and into India. Nifty Bank rose 2.49% to a record close of 64,635.20, per Liquide and Marketcalls, and Nifty IT rose 1.30%, per Kotak Neo. More shares fell than rose on the BSE (1,582 up, 2,456 down), per HDFC Sky. Apollo Hospitals (+5.29%) and Axis Bank (+4.72%) gained the most in the Nifty; Titan (−1.70%) and Tata Steel (−1.60%) fell the most, per HDFC Sky.
