How Newspin works

News is written for people who already know the background. Newspin is for everyone else: each big story becomes one timeline you can read in 30 seconds or study in depth.

Three layers on every story

In 30 seconds: what happened, why it matters and what’s next. Timeline: the key events in order. Detailed: open any event for the numbers, the original documents and every outlet that reported it.

How we source

  • Every event links to the reports it is based on. Where an official document exists (a regulator’s statement, a gazette notification, official statistics), we link it and mark it primary.
  • We prefer official sources, then established news outlets. Analyst and economist views are labelled Forecast and never presented as fact.
  • When an event rests on a single outlet, we say so.
  • When reliable sources disagree, we show a Sources differ note instead of quietly picking one.
  • We summarise in our own words and link to the original. We don’t republish articles.

How timelines are built

AI tools help us gather reports and draft events. A person checks every event, its date and its sources before it is published.

Corrections

If something is wrong or missing, email hello@newspinapp.com with the story and the event. We fix errors quickly and note significant corrections on the timeline.

Independence

Newspin is independent and not affiliated with any political party or news outlet. Story selection is based on how big and long-running a story is, not on who it helps.
