    Inflation is back: India’s climb from record lows

    Retail inflation fell to its lowest ever in late 2025. By October 2026 it had crossed the RBI's 6% upper limit. Here is how it happened.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    Retail inflation rose to 6.09% in October 2026, above the RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit for the first time in two years. Food inflation reached 8.94%. Wholesale inflation rose to 12.29%, pushed up by fuel and power prices.

    Why it matters

    Higher prices for food and fuel squeeze household budgets. Inflation above 6% puts pressure on the RBI to raise rates again.

    What’s next

    The RBI's next policy decision comes after its 2–4 Dec meeting. November inflation is due on 12 December.

    9. 16 Nov2026
      16 Nov 2026 Data New since your last visit Official data · Commerce Ministry

      Wholesale inflation rises to 12.29%, led by fuel and power

      Wholesale price () inflation, which tracks prices businesses pay in bulk, rises to 12.29% in October from 11.39% in September, provisional government data show. Fuel and power prices are up 32.84% from a year earlier (25.85% in September), driven by mineral oils, crude petroleum, natural gas and electricity. Inflation in manufactured products eases slightly to 9.54%, and the food index to 8.58%. Higher wholesale costs can feed into shop prices over time.
