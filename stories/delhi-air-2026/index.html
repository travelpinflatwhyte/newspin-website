Delhi's air in winter 2026: every stage, every spike

From a clean October day to Diwali smog and Stage III curbs. How Delhi's air turned this season, and what the rules mean for you.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Delhi's air turned 'severe' on 13 November, with AQI at 403, then eased to 349 ('very poor') on the morning of 14 November. Curbs moved from Stage I on 21 October to Stage III on 6 November, and Diwali brought the worst festival-day air since 2021.

Why it matters

Air in the 'very poor' and 'severe' bands harms everyone's lungs, especially children, older people and anyone with asthma or heart disease. Each GRAP stage adds limits on construction, vehicles and burning.

What’s next

The IMD says light rain or drizzle and stronger winds are possible on Sunday 15 November, which may help clear the air. Stage III stays until the air commission says otherwise. Out-of-state vehicles that are not BS-VI are barred from Delhi until 31 January 2027.

What this means for you

Check the AQI before going out, limit outdoor exercise on 'very poor' and 'severe' days, and follow the official CAQM orders for what Stage III restricts. If your car is registered outside Delhi and is not BS-VI, it can be turned back at the border until 31 January.
