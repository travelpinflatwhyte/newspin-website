New National Electricity Policy: what it could mean for your power bill

A draft policy to replace the 2005 rules proposes yearly automatic tariff increases and cutting cross-subsidies.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

The Power Ministry released a draft National Electricity Policy in January 2026 to replace the 2005 policy. Public comments closed on 19 February.

Why it matters

It proposes index-linked tariff increases every April and limits on cross-subsidies, which could change household and industrial power bills.

What’s next

The final policy has to be notified. Nothing in the draft applies until then.

What this means for you

Under the draft, tariffs would rise each April by an index rather than waiting for state regulators, and free power would need the subsidy paid to discoms in advance.
