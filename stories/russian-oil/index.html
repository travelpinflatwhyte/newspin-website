    India’s Russian oil dilemma

    India promised Washington it would stop buying Russian oil. Then war closed the Gulf. Now the Gulf is back on top and the US tariff threat is on hold.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    Russian crude filled the gap when was disrupted. In October the US waived its threatened on big buyers of Russian oil, and in November India's imports from the Gulf overtook Russia's for the first time since the Iran war began.

    Why it matters

    Cheap Russian oil cushions India's import bill; being punished for it would raise costs and strain relations with the US.

    What’s next

    The US waiver is temporary. India is on course for record Russian oil imports in 2026, Reuters reports, but refiners expect to buy less in 2027 if Gulf supplies return to normal.

    8. 24 Nov2026
      24 Nov 2026 Trade New since your last visit Market data · Kpler, trade sources (via Reuters)

      India on course for record Russian oil imports in 2026, but refiners expect less next year

      India imported an average of 1.99 million barrels a day of Russian crude this year up to early November, about 250,000 more than a year earlier, according to Kpler data cited by Reuters. Trade sources told Reuters that Russian oil made up about 38% of India's imports from January to October. Refiners expect a full-year average of about 1.9 million barrels a day. India and China together are buying more than 80% of Russia's crude exports, two industry sources said. Indian refiners told Reuters their purchases are likely to fall back to pre-Iran-war levels in 2027 if Gulf supplies return to normal, with price deciding how much they buy.

    9. 25 Nov2026
      25 Nov 2026 Trade New since your last visit Reported · Bloomberg; Market data · Kpler

      Russian oil loses its price edge in India as Gulf supplies return, Bloomberg reports

      Russian crude has lost much of its price advantage in India as Middle East exports recover, Bloomberg reported, according to AzerNews and UA.News. Kpler analysts expect India's Russian purchases to fall to about 1.6 to 1.7 million barrels a day by the end of November, about 40% below a record of 2.8 million in July, per UA.News citing Bloomberg. Imports from the Middle East reached about 2.1 million barrels a day in early November. Brazilian supplies are set to rise to about 400,000 barrels a day in November from about 100,000 in October. Russian Urals crude for late-December and January delivery is offered at a discount of $2 to $3 a barrel to , up from $1 to $2 a few weeks earlier, per Reuters figures cited by AzerNews. Higher shipping costs and competition from other suppliers have eroded Russia's edge, per Bloomberg.
