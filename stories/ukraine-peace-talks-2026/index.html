    Russia–Ukraine peace talks: from Abu Dhabi to the Crimean bridge strikes

    US-brokered talks restarted in November after nine months and agreed a big prisoner swap. Then Ukraine hit the Crimean bridge, Putin hardened his line and Russia struck Kyiv's bridges.

    In 30 seconds

    For everyone

    What happened

    The US brokered three-way talks with Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi in January and February 2026, then they stalled for nine months. They resumed on 10–11 November and agreed a large prisoner exchange, but not the main dispute: Russia wants all of Ukraine's Donbas region. In the week after, Ukraine struck the bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea three times. On 20 November Russia hit three bridges in Kyiv; Ukraine said eight people were killed.

    Why it matters

    It is Europe's largest war since 1945. Its course affects energy and food prices, on Russia (including on the oil India buys) and European security.

    What’s next

    No date is set for the next round. The Kremlin says lists for a "truly large-scale" prisoner exchange are being drawn up with mediators, and Zelenskyy says Ukraine is preparing it; Kyiv expects the first stage in late November or early December. The Kremlin says the peace process "isn't going along smoothly" and blames Europe. Russian missiles and drones hit Kyiv, Kharkiv and Kryvyi Rih on 25–26 November. Trump says he asked Putin to pull back military aggression across Europe; the Kremlin has not said whether Putin will attend the summit in Florida on 14–15 December.

    1. 23–24 Jan2026
      23–24 Jan 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · US, Russian and Ukrainian delegations

      First US–Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi end without a deal

      The United Arab Emirates hosted the first three-way talks, brokered by the US. Russia's team was led by its military intelligence chief and Ukraine's by Rustem Umerov. There was no agreement: Russia demanded that Ukraine withdraw fully from the Donbas region in the east, and Kyiv refused. US officials said the talks 'surpassed expectations'. On the night they ended, Russia launched more than 370 drones and 21 missiles at Ukraine, per CNN.

    2. 4–5 Feb2026
      4–5 Feb 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Witkoff, Russian Defence Ministry

      Second round: no ceasefire, but 314 prisoners of war swapped

      A second round in Abu Dhabi produced no ceasefire; control of the Donetsk region remained the main dispute. US envoy Steve Witkoff said the sides agreed to exchange 314 prisoners of war, and Russia's RIA news agency reported 157 returned by each side. It was the first swap since October 2025. Talks then stalled and lapsed after the US and Israel launched a war on Iran, Al Jazeera and Reuters report.

    5. 10–11 Nov2026
      10–11 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Russian and Ukrainian delegations

      First three-way talks in nine months: a big prisoner swap agreed, no breakthrough

      Kushner and Witkoff mediated, with Vladimir Medinsky leading Russia's team and Umerov Ukraine's. Umerov said the main result was agreement on a 'large-scale prisoner exchange'; Medinsky called it a 'fundamental agreement'. A Russian general quoted by the TASS news agency said it would involve more than 1,000 people on each side. Kushner said the gaps had 'narrowed', but the sides still disagreed on territory, including Russia's demand for all of Donbas.

      Ukrainian strike brings down part of the Crimean bridge road

      Ukraine hit the 19-km Kerch bridge, which links Russia to occupied Crimea, with drones and missiles. Zelenskyy called it an 'unprecedented operation in response to Russian attacks on our infrastructure'. Al Jazeera reports about 200 metres of roadway heavily damaged and partly underwater. Russia opened a terrorism case, and the Kremlin said the bridge would be restored. Russian strikes damaged two bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv the same day.

    11. 19–20 Nov2026
      19–20 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Stated · Kushner; Stated · Russian Foreign Ministry

      Kushner says Russia accepts Europe at the table; Moscow rejects any NATO-style role for Kyiv

      After the Berlin meeting of Ukraine's allies, US envoy Jared Kushner said: 'I think we've gotten the Russians comfortable with that concept where any security guarantee for Ukraine would be based on .' Article 5 is NATO's rule that an attack on one member is an attack on all. He also said Russia had agreed to talks that include Europe. Moscow has not confirmed this. Russian Deputy Foreign Minister Sergei Ryabkov said any 'quasi membership' of NATO for Kyiv was 'totally unacceptable', and that a visit by European representatives was 'under consideration'. Zelenskyy said the next meeting would be four-way: Ukraine, the US, Russia and Europe. No date has been set.

    12. 20 Nov2026
      20 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Kyiv city authorities, Ukrainian government, Russian Defence Ministry

      Russia strikes three Kyiv bridges after Putin promises a response

      Overnight Russian ballistic missiles damaged the Podilskyi, Darnytskyi and Southern bridges over the Dnipro in Kyiv, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said. Traffic was restricted and people crossed the river on foot. Prime Minister Koretskyi said eight people were killed, six of them in Kyiv, and 28 wounded. Russia's Defence Ministry said it targeted bridges used to move weapons, plus defence, energy and transport sites. Power was cut across Kyiv and in 10 regions after nuclear plants reduced output, Ukraine's energy ministry said. Putin had said in China: 'We will respond to these attacks.'

    14. 22 Nov2026
      22 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Confirmed · Ukrainian officials (casualties); Stated · Zelenskyy (weekly totals)

      Russian strikes on Kyiv region hit an apartment block and a supermarket; Zelenskyy asks the US for Patriot missiles

      A Russian strike early on Sunday destroyed part of a five-storey apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv. At least three people were killed and four wounded, per AP and the Kyiv regional governor via Ukrinform, and about a dozen were still believed trapped. On Sunday evening a drone hit a supermarket in the Vyshneve area of Kyiv region, killing at least two people and injuring eight, the governor said, per the Kyiv Independent. He declared 23 November a day of mourning in the region. Zelenskyy said Russia launched about 2,700 attack drones, nearly 1,460 guided bombs and 165 missiles over the past week, and urged the US to approve delivery of Patriot air-defence missiles that partners have already paid for, per Al Jazeera. No new talks or prisoner exchange were reported.

    15. 22 Nov2026
      22 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Stated · Zelenskyy, SBU

      Ukraine says its sea drones hit a Russian warship in a Libyan port, 2,400 km from the front

      President Zelenskyy said on 23 November that Ukrainian sea drones hit the Russian corvette Merkury at a naval base in Benghazi, eastern Libya, on Sunday 22 November. He said seven drones were used, a helicopter on board was destroyed, and a cargo ship, the Angara, was also hit; he said Russia used it to move weapons. Ukraine's security service (SBU) said the drones were launched from a "mother ship". At least 10 people were wounded, including Libyans and Russians, per AP. Libya's eastern government asked the UN for an investigation. The Kremlin said it had "taken note". The site is about 2,400 km from the front line. Separately, a Russian oil refinery in Rostov stopped work after a Ukrainian drone attack, its regional governor said.

    19. 24–25 Nov2026
      24–25 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Stated · Ukrainian government (strikes, casualties); Stated · Budanov (swap timing)

      Russian strikes hit power and gas sites in several regions; Kyiv says the first prisoners could come home by early December

      Overnight into 25 November Russia launched 112 attack drones, of which 88 were shot down or jammed, Prime Minister Serhii Koretskyi said, per LB.ua. Strikes damaged a DTEK thermal power plant, halting generation, and set a Naftogaz gas production site in Poltava region on fire, per Reuters. Power was cut in parts of Odesa and Zaporizhzhia, and outside power to the occupied Zaporizhzhia nuclear plant was lost for the 17th time this year, a deputy energy minister said. One person was killed in Kyiv region and six were injured in Zaporizhzhia. Separately, Kyrylo Budanov, head of Zelenskyy's office, said the first prisoners in the swap agreed in Abu Dhabi could return in late November or early December, in several stages, per Xinhua citing Interfax-Ukraine. Ukraine's prisoner-of-war office said it is ready for the exchange; the final number has not been set.

    20. 25–26 Nov2026
      25–26 Nov 2026 World New since your last visit Stated · Ukrainian air force and officials (strikes); Stated · Kremlin, Zelenskyy (swap)

      Russian missiles hit a Kyiv shopping centre; Kremlin and Zelenskyy say a large prisoner swap is being prepared

      Overnight into 26 November Russia launched ballistic missiles and 113 attack drones, Ukraine's air force said; 87 drones were shot down or jammed. Missiles hit a shopping centre and an industrial building in Kyiv, injuring seven people, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said, per Euromaidan Press. In Kharkiv a drone set fire to a flat in a high-rise, and two people were killed, per Kyiv Post. Strikes also caused power problems in Kryvyi Rih. On 26 November Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Russian and Ukrainian security services were drawing up lists with mediators under the Abu Dhabi agreements, and he hoped a "truly large-scale exchange" would take place soon, per TASS. He also said "the peace process isn't going along smoothly", blaming European support for Kyiv. In his evening address Zelenskyy said Ukraine was preparing the next exchange and hoped it would be "substantial". No date or final number has been given.
