Digi Yatra goes international

Face-based airport entry and boarding, already used at 39 domestic airports, is now on trial for overseas departures.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Since 1 November 2026, Indian e-passport holders on select Air India and IndiGo international flights can use Digi Yatra at seven airports.

Why it matters

It could make international departures quicker, but it does not replace passport, airline or immigration checks.

What’s next

The trial runs until December 2026. Feedback will shape a wider rollout in 2027.

What this means for you

To try it you need an Indian e-passport, the Digi Yatra app and a select Air India or IndiGo international flight from Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi, Mumbai, Chennai or Amritsar. Carry your documents as usual.
