Every major AI model release, 2025–2026

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI and China's labs: which model came out when, in one timeline.

In 30 seconds

For everyone

What happened

Since January 2025, the leading labs have released a major model roughly every two weeks. OpenAI moved from GPT-4.5 to GPT-6, Anthropic from Claude 4 to Fable 5.2, Google from Gemini 3 to Gemini 4, Meta from Llama to Muse, and Chinese labs closed much of the gap with open models.

Why it matters

Each release changes which AI tools are best and cheapest for writing, coding, search and work. Launches often land within days of each other as companies compete.

What’s next

Grok 5, Qwen 4 and DeepSeek V4.1 Pro are all promised without dates.
